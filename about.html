Our Mission & Engineering Culture

Engineering Truth in
Conversational AI

We founded Isomorphic AI with a clear conviction: enterprise conversational AI must be deterministic, respect proprietary tenant privacy, and bridge directly into operational customer support workflows.

🎯

Deterministic Grounding First

We refuse probabilistic guessing. When an enterprise user asks a question, our RAG pipeline either retrieves verified document chunks with explicit citations or gracefully offers ticket generation.

🎫

Integrated Support Workflows

A chatbot shouldn't be an isolated island. By building native grievance escalation into the chat interface and feeding it directly to a tenant admin portal, customer issues never slip through cracks.

🛡️

Uncompromising Vector Privacy

Enterprise data is an organization's most valuable asset. Every customer receives dedicated vector partitions, strict PII redaction, and a guarantee of zero third-party foundation model training.
