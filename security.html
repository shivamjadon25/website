Zero-Trust Infrastructure

The 6 Pillars of Isomorphic AI Security

Unlike generic wrapper tools, our architecture is engineered from the ground up for strict organizational confidentiality and regulatory compliance.

🔒

Dedicated Vector Partitions

100% logical and physical isolation of vector embeddings. Tenant data is isolated in dedicated database partitions with cryptographic tenant keys.

🚫

Zero Public Model Training

Your documentation, internal manuals, and user transcripts are never shared, sold, or used to train third-party foundation models.

🎯

Deterministic Grounding

Responses are mathematically constrained to approved source documents with strict cosine similarity thresholds and exact clause citations.

🛡️

Automated PII Masking

Named Entity Recognition (NER) and regex engines automatically sanitize credit cards, phone numbers, and sensitive IDs prior to indexing.

🔐

Prompt Injection Defense

Multi-layered semantic firewalls detect and block jailbreak attempts, system prompt overrides, and adversarial payload injections.

🔑

End-to-End Encryption

All data in transit is secured via TLS 1.3 with Perfect Forward Secrecy, while vector partitions and archives use AES-256 at rest.
